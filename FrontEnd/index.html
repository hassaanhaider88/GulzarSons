What is Furniture?

Furniture refers to movable objects that are used to support various human activities such as Seating (e.g., chairs, sofas) Eating (e.g., tables) Sleeping (e.g., beds) Storing items (e.g., cupboards, shelves, wardrobes) Working (e.g., desks) Furniture can be made from a variety of materials like wood, metal, plastic, and fabric, and it often combines function with design to enhance the look and utility of living or working spaces.
